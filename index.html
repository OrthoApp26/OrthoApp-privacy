Features

Everything an orthopedic practice records, in one place

Designed around the way surgeons actually follow patients, from first visit to long-term follow-up.

Comprehensive patient profiles

Track demographics, comorbidities and the full course of each patient in clearly organized tabs, so nothing gets lost between visits.

Specialized orthopedic tracking

Dedicated sections for Surgery, Clinical evaluation with validated scoring systems and classifications, Labs, and Radiology.

  • Surgery, implants, complications and revisions
  • Scores and classifications for follow-up visits
  • Laboratory panels with trends

Secure and offline-first

Built on local storage (IndexedDB), so patient data stays on your device. There is no server to breach, no account to create, and it works in the operating theatre or the clinic with no signal.

Advanced data management

Store radiographs with the patient, export a complete patient sheet to PDF, and back up everything with a single file. Restore on a new phone, optionally with password-protected AES-256 encryption.

  • Radiographs and report photos saved on-device
  • PDF patient sheets · CSV data exports
  • Backup and restore in one file
Interface

Fast, familiar and made for one-handed use

Move through a patient file with seamless tab navigation. Tap a tab to preview it.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

No sign-up, no setup, no internet connection required.

Install the app

Download OrthoApp from Google Play and open it. Everything is ready straight away.

Add patients and records

Create a patient sheet, then record surgery, scores, labs and radiographs as you go.

Export and back up

Create a PDF patient sheet or a CSV export, and save a backup file whenever you like.

Your patients' data stays with you

OrthoApp has no cloud, no accounts and no analytics. Information is stored in the app on your device, and you decide when to export or back it up.
