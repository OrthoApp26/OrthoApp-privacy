OrthoApp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Last updated: 10 October 2026

OrthoApp ("the app") is an orthopedic patient-recording, radiology-measurement, scoring and laboratory-tracking tool for medical professionals. The app is published by Bassem Ehab Abdelbarey Ghoneim ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what we do, and do not do, with it.

1. The short version

  • OrthoApp works offline. The information you enter stays on your own device.
  • We do not run servers that receive your data. We cannot see, read, copy or sell your patient records.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You are in control of your data: you can export it, back it up, and delete it at any time.

2. Information you enter into the app

Depending on how you use it, you may enter patient details (such as name, medical record number, age, sex, diagnosis, notes), visit records, scoring results, laboratory results and photographs of laboratory reports, and radiographs or other images used for measurements. This is sensitive health information.

All of it is stored only in the private storage of the app on your device (an on-device database). It is not uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers.

4. Backups and exports you create

The app lets you save backup files, PDF reports and data exports to your device or share them through other apps you choose (for example email or cloud storage). These files are created only when you ask for them, and where they go is your decision. Once a file leaves the app, it is under the control of the app or service you sent it to, and our policy no longer applies to it.

Backup files can be protected with a password (AES-256 encryption). We cannot recover a lost password, and we cannot decrypt your backups.

5. Purchases

The free version can be upgraded to OrthoApp Pro through a subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and shares with us only the information needed to confirm that your subscription is active. We never see or store your card details. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Links to other websites

Some scoring tools contain links to external reference websites (for example AO Foundation and OrthoToolkit). They open in your browser and are operated by others. No patient data is sent when you open them. Please read their own privacy policies.

7. Permissions

The app only asks for access that you trigger yourself, such as choosing an image file or saving and sharing a file. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, or messages.

8. How we protect data

Data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's own security. The app disables Android's automatic cloud backup of its data, so its contents are not copied to Google Drive without your action. You are responsible for keeping your device secure (screen lock, device encryption, updates) and for keeping your backup files safe.

9. Your responsibilities for patient data

If you record real patients' information, you are the person responsible for it under the laws and professional rules that apply to you (for example patient confidentiality, informed consent and data-protection laws such as Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, the EU GDPR, or HIPAA where relevant). Please obtain any required consent, and remove or anonymize identifying details when you export data for research or sharing. The app is a tool; it does not make you compliant by itself.

10. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual patients and records inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently removes all of its data from the device. Because we do not hold copies, we cannot restore deleted data. Make a backup before deleting or changing phones.

11. Children

OrthoApp is a professional tool for medical practitioners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Medical disclaimer

OrthoApp is a reference and record-keeping aid. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional clinical judgment. Always verify measurements, scores and classifications before making clinical decisions.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example if a future version adds online features), we will update the date above and describe the change in the app or the Google Play listing before it takes effect.
